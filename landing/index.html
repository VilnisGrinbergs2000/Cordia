Why we ask

Only two things here actually cost money: storing the files people upload, and moving voice, video, and messages between them. Everything else is close to free at our size. Tips cover those two bills, which is why nothing has to sit behind a paywall and why there are no ads to fall back on.

About supporting Cordia

Is the free plan a trial?

No. Free is the product, not a countdown. It has no end date, no card on file, and nothing to cancel. Every feature on this page is in it.

What do I get for tipping?

A Supporter badge on your profile, and our thanks. Nothing is unlocked, because nothing was locked to begin with. Tips are one-off, so there is no subscription to manage or cancel.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No. There are no ads and there never will be, and your data is not for sale. Tips exist so the project does not have to depend on either one.

Who processes the payment?

Buy Me a Coffee, on their own site. Your card details never reach Cordia, and we are not told what you paid — only that someone did.

A few good questions

Before you dive in.

Codi

Ask me anything.

Is Cordia free to use?

Cordia is free to download and use. Create an account and join a community on Cordia.

Are my messages private?

Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted with X3DH and the Double Ratchet. Server channels are visible to members with access to that channel, so share accordingly.

What do I need to run Cordia?

The desktop app runs on Windows 10/11 or a modern x64 Linux desktop. The web app needs a current browser and a Cordia account.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — open Cordia in your phone’s browser and add it to your home screen as a web app. Native Android and iOS builds are in the works.

Does Cordia work offline?

The app shell can load without a connection, but messages, calls, and live updates need the server. Background push alerts are available in the installed web app.
